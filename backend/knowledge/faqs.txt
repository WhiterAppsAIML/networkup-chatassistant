NETWORKUP.IO FAQ KNOWLEDGE BASE
Source: https://networkup.io/
Prepared from the current NetworkUp.io website content.

========================================
1. ABOUT NETWORKUP
========================================

Q: What is NetworkUp.io?

A:
NetworkUp.io is an AI-powered LinkedIn growth and outreach platform designed to help users automate LinkedIn networking, reach relevant decision-makers, manage conversations, and turn connections into opportunities.

Q: Who is NetworkUp built for?

A:
NetworkUp is positioned for sales teams, recruiters, agencies, founders, consultants, B2B marketing teams, SaaS companies, enterprise teams, revenue teams, fractional CMOs, SDRs and BDRs.

Q: What does NetworkUp help users do?

A:
NetworkUp helps users find relevant prospects, automate outreach, manage LinkedIn conversations, build campaigns, analyze campaign performance, enrich leads, and identify high-intent opportunities.

========================================
2. HOW NETWORKUP WORKS
========================================

Q: How does the NetworkUp workflow work?

A:
The website describes a three-step workflow:
1. Define your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
2. Launch multi-step sequences with personalized messaging.
3. Convert replies to revenue and sync interactions back to the CRM.

Q: Can I create automated LinkedIn campaigns?

A:
Yes. NetworkUp provides a Campaign Builder with drag-and-drop workflow sequences, intelligent branching logic and delays.

Q: Can I personalize outreach messages?

A:
Yes. NetworkUp supports personalized messaging, and its Growth plan lists advanced AI personalization.

Q: Can I use multi-step sequences?

A:
Yes. NetworkUp supports multi-step sequences for connecting, following up and nurturing prospects.

========================================
3. SMART PROSPECTING
========================================

Q: What is Smart Prospecting?

A:
Smart Prospecting uses AI filters to identify high-intent leads based on real-time professional signals.

Q: How does NetworkUp help find leads?

A:
NetworkUp uses AI-assisted prospecting to identify leads that match a user's ideal customer profile and to surface high-intent opportunities.

Q: What is an Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)?

A:
An ICP describes the characteristics and criteria of the type of customer or prospect a business wants to target.

========================================
4. CAMPAIGNS & AUTOMATION
========================================

Q: What is Campaign Builder?

A:
Campaign Builder is a drag-and-drop workflow tool for creating outreach sequences with intelligent branching logic and delays.

Q: Can NetworkUp optimize campaigns?

A:
Yes. The website says its AI can analyze campaign performance and suggest improvements. It also lists an AI Campaign Optimizer that analyzes campaigns before launch and recommends improvements intended to improve acceptance and reply rates.

Q: What is Campaign Simulator?

A:
Campaign Simulator is described as a tool that predicts campaign performance before launch using AI insights and historical outreach data.

Q: Can NetworkUp automate follow-ups?

A:
Yes. The website describes multi-step sequences for connecting, following up and nurturing with personalized messaging. The site also announces Smart AI Follow-ups as a feature.

========================================
5. LINKEDIN SAFETY
========================================

Q: Does NetworkUp have account protection?

A:
Yes. NetworkUp lists Account Protection and describes proprietary safety systems intended to keep LinkedIn accounts secure and active.

Q: Is NetworkUp guaranteed to prevent LinkedIn restrictions?

A:
The website describes safety and account-protection systems, but it should not be treated as a guarantee that an account can never be restricted. Users should follow LinkedIn's policies and use automation responsibly.

Q: What should I do if I have a LinkedIn account-safety concern?

A:
Review account-protection and campaign settings, follow LinkedIn's policies, and contact NetworkUp support when additional assistance is needed.

11. PRICING & PLANS
========================================

Q: What is the NetworkUp Starter plan?

A:
Starter is listed at $21/month. It includes 1 LinkedIn account, 500 messages per month, daily activity reports, basic personalization, a basic lead database and email support.

Q: What is the NetworkUp Growth plan?

A:
Growth is listed at $59/month. It includes 5 LinkedIn accounts, multi-workspace, advanced AI personalization, sequence branching, 2-variant A/B testing, full CRM sync, a read-only API, an extended database, a shared success manager, and email and chat support.

Q: What is the NetworkUp Enterprise plan?

A:
Enterprise is listed at $129/month. It includes custom organization limits, SSO/SAML setup, a custom logic engine, regional compliance, priority execution, custom webhooks, an on-prem hosting option, audit logging, 24/7 priority support and technical onboarding.

Q: Does NetworkUp offer yearly billing?

A:
The pricing section displays a yearly option marked as 20% off.

Q: Does NetworkUp offer a free trial?

A:
The website advertises a 14-day free trial and states that no credit card is required to start.

Q: Does NetworkUp offer agency or enterprise support?

A:
Yes. The Enterprise tier is designed for organizations at scale and includes technical onboarding, priority support, custom integrations and other enterprise-oriented capabilities.
