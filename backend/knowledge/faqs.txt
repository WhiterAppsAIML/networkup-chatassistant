NETWORKUP / LINKEDIN AUTOMATION FAQ

========================================
1. GETTING STARTED
========================================

Q: What is LinkedIn automation?

A:
LinkedIn automation helps users automate repetitive outreach activities such as finding prospects, sending connection requests, following up with leads, and managing outreach campaigns.

Q: How does the platform work?

A:
The typical workflow is:
1. Connect a LinkedIn account.
2. Create and configure a campaign.
3. Add or find prospects.
4. Start the campaign.
5. Monitor campaign activity and responses.

Q: How do I create my first campaign?

A:
Create a campaign, select the target prospects, configure the outreach sequence and personalization, review the settings, and start the campaign.

Q: Can I manage multiple LinkedIn accounts?

A:
The platform is designed to support multi-account outreach and account management. Exact account limits depend on the plan and configuration.

========================================
2. LINKEDIN SAFETY
========================================

Q: Is LinkedIn automation safe?

A:
LinkedIn automation should be configured carefully and used within appropriate platform limits. Recommended safety settings, pacing and account limits should be followed to reduce the risk of account restrictions.

Q: Can LinkedIn restrict an account?

A:
Yes. LinkedIn may restrict accounts that perform activity that violates its policies or appears unusual. Users should follow LinkedIn's rules and use conservative automation settings.

Q: Can I pause a campaign?

A:
Campaigns should be paused when necessary to stop automated outreach. The exact pause controls depend on the platform interface.

Q: What should I do if LinkedIn asks for verification?

A:
Pause automated activity and complete the required LinkedIn verification before continuing.

10. TROUBLESHOOTING
========================================

Q: Why is my campaign paused?

A:
Check the campaign status, account connection, configured limits and any warnings shown by the platform.

Q: Why are invitations not being sent?

A:
Check whether the campaign is active, the LinkedIn account is connected, daily limits have been reached, or the campaign has encountered an error.

Q: Why are messages delayed?

A:
Messages may be delayed because of configured schedules, pacing, campaign limits or account conditions.

Q: Why can't I connect my LinkedIn account?

A:
Check the account credentials, connection status, browser/session state and any verification requirements.

Q: What should I do if prospects are missing?

A:
Check the selected lead source, filters, imports, campaign targeting and whether the prospects meet the configured criteria.
