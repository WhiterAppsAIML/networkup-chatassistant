Reachy vs Meet Alfred (2026)

Meet Alfred is presented as a multichannel cloud tool, while Reachy is presented as a local AI agent focused deeply on LinkedIn.

Key comparison:
Reachy runs on the user's machine and IP; Meet Alfred runs in the cloud.
Reachy pricing: Free to $39/month. Meet Alfred: about $39–59/month per seat.
Reachy focuses on deep LinkedIn functionality with CRM sync; Meet Alfred supports LinkedIn, email and X.
Reachy sources leads from signals such as posts, groups, followers and Slack; Meet Alfred uses search-based sourcing.
Reachy supports GPT-5, Claude Fable 5 and Gemini using the user's own key with 0% markup; Meet Alfred uses built-in templates.
Reachy includes lead scoring; the comparison says Meet Alfred does not.
Reachy has a 14-day trial plus free monitoring; Meet Alfred is described as trial-only.

The article says Meet Alfred's strength is breadth across LinkedIn, email and X, with a large template library.
Reachy's difference is local LinkedIn automation, signal-based sourcing, ICP scoring, Open Profile InMails, company messaging, multi-account rotation and warm-up. It also says Reachy can sync enriched contacts to HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Make, Zapier and n8n.
The article recommends Meet Alfred for multichannel breadth and Reachy when LinkedIn is the main pipeline and local execution, targeting and safety are priorities.
